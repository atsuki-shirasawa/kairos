<section class="kx-setup" aria-labelledby="kx-setup-title">
  <h2 id="kx-setup-title">On your Mac, from your own logs</h2>
  <p>
    Kairos reads Claude Code's logs into SQLite on your Mac and never writes them, so your history
    outlives Claude Code's log retention. Each work block is summarized in the background with
    <code>claude -p</code>, through your own login; those excerpts are all that leave your Mac.
  </p>
